# Research log

One entry per working session, newest first. Write it yourself, in your own words, the same day. It is the record your supervisor, reviewers and future you will trust: what you ran, what came out, what you concluded, and what you will do next.

Template:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD: <one-line title>

**Ran:** command(s), model, settings, run folder
**Result:** the numbers, with the run folder they came from
**Observed:** 2–3 trajectories you actually read, and what went wrong in them
**Conclusion:** what you now believe, and how confident you are
**Next:** the single next experiment
```
